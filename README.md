# IBM HR Attrition Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using **Power BI** to identify patterns and factors associated with employee turnover.

The analysis provides an interactive view of employee demographics, job-related factors, compensation, satisfaction, and other attributes that can help HR teams understand attrition trends and support data-driven workforce decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Identify patterns among employees who left the organization
- Understand how attrition varies across different employee and job characteristics
- Explore factors that may be associated with higher employee turnover
- Present insights through an interactive HR analytics dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculations and analytical measures
- **Microsoft Excel** – Source dataset

---

## 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of employee attrition across different dimensions, including:

- Employee demographics
- Department and job roles
- Job satisfaction
- Monthly income
- Years at company
- Work experience
- Education
- Business travel
- Overtime
- Other employee-related factors

Interactive filters and visualizations allow users to explore the data and identify patterns in employee attrition.

---

## 💡 Key Analysis Areas

The project focuses on understanding questions such as:

- Which employee groups experience higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary across departments and job roles?
- Is there a relationship between overtime and employee attrition?
- How does compensation relate to attrition?
- How does employee experience affect turnover?
- Which demographic and job-related factors are associated with employee attrition?
